# Pixovela

Pixovela is a fast, modern alternative to the default Windows image viewer. Open a photo, flip through the folder with the arrow keys, or find the shot you remember by typing something like “a vacation by the sea.”

**[Download Pixovela for Windows](../../releases)**

> The Pixovela installer is currently unsigned, so Windows may show a SmartScreen warning or block installation, depending on your settings. Download Pixovela only from this repository’s Releases page.

## Why I built Pixovela

I’m a 3D artist by trade, and photography is one of my many hobbies. Like a lot of amateur photographers, I’ve ended up with thousands of JPEGs, RAW files, and videos. Whenever I sat down to go through them in the default Windows viewer, I kept running into the same problems. Colors would shift, browsing would slow down, or the viewer would glitch. All I wanted was to open a photo and keep going.

Recent advances in AI gave me the confidence to try building a small, simple viewer for myself. I’m new to software development, but working with images has given me a clear idea of what I want from a viewer. Pixovela has grown since that first idea, and there’s still plenty I’d like to add. I’m proud of how far it’s come as my first app.

— [jadestudio-max](https://github.com/jadestudio-max)

## What you can do

- **Browse photos and camera RAW files** with the arrow keys or **WASD**. Switch to fullscreen, fit the photo to your screen, or zoom in at the cursor and check details at 100%.
- **Bring multiple folders into one library.** Browse photos by year or month, switch between All photos, Standard photos, and RAW only, or hide documents and screenshots.
- **Find photos with local AI search.** Type a phrase like “a dog running on the beach” or search for someone by the name you’ve saved in People.
- **Organize your library.** Create albums, name people, and merge duplicate face groups. Password-protected albums hide their photos across Pixovela while keeping the original files in place.
- **Play fullscreen slideshows** with smooth transitions, gentle motion, and adjustable timing. Portrait photos can appear together in groups of three.
- **Watch local videos and inspect them frame by frame.** Pause on a moment and step forward or backward through the footage.
- **Open the original photo in your preferred editor** or jump straight to its folder in File Explorer.
- **Preview photos before printing.** See the paper layout, margins, and orientation before sending a photo to your printer.

## Install

Download the `.exe` installer from [Releases](../../releases). Pixovela runs on 64-bit Windows 10 (version 2004 or later) and Windows 11.
